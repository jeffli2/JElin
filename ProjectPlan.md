Overview: 

In the world, not every country performs the same. Some countries like the U.S., Britain, and Japan are known to be very economically powerful and wealthy given their large overall GDP and nice wages. With these countries being wealthy and economically prosperous, does that necessarily mean that the people there are happier than those countries with lower wages and smaller GDP size due to patterns? However, we should not make these assumptions just yet. Not every country is perfect and maybe some countries that may appear wealthy might have unsatisfied citizens. Thus, this is why we decided to focus our project on this issue so that we can make a more precise statement backed by data analysis.

Research Question(s): 

Does an overall country’s GDP and minimal wage determine the level of happiness for their citizens?
